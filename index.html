3. How Calculations Work (Formulas & Verification)

Transparent mathematical breakdown of Current %, Future %, and Attendance Planning

1. Current Subject Attendance %

$$\text{Attendance \%} = \left(\frac{\text{Classes Attended}}{\text{Total Classes Conducted}}\right) \times 100$$

Verification: $\text{Total Conducted} = \text{Attended} + \text{Absent}$. Auto-verified in real-time.

2. Predicted Future Attendance %

$$\text{Future \%} = \left(\frac{\text{Current Attended} + \text{Future Attended}}{\text{Current Total} + \text{Future Attended} + \text{Future Missed}}\right) \times 100$$

Live Simulator: Test different scenarios of upcoming classes you plan to attend or miss.

3. Classes Needed to Reach Target ($R\%$)

$$\text{Classes Needed } (x) = \left\lceil \frac{R \cdot \text{Total} - 100 \cdot \text{Attended}}{100 - R} \right\rceil$$

Consecutive upcoming classes you must attend (without missing any) to pull your attendance up to $R\%$.

4. Safe Classes You Can Miss / Bunk

$$\text{Safe Bunks } (y) = \left\lfloor \frac{100 \cdot \text{Attended} - R \cdot \text{Total}}{R} \right\rfloor$$

Maximum upcoming classes you can miss while still maintaining at least $R\%$ attendance.

5. Overall Weighted Attendance %

$$\text{Overall Attendance \%} = \left(\frac{\sum \text{Attended in all subjects}}{\sum \text{Total Conducted in all subjects}}\right) \times 100$$

Crucial Note: Calculated across total conducted hours, never by simply averaging subject percentages (which gives mathematically incorrect results when subjects have different class counts).

B.Tech CSE Example: Data Structures (Requested Demo)

Current State
  • Total classes conducted = 40
  • Classes attended = 30
  • Classes absent = 10 ($40 - 30 = 10$)
  • Current Attendance % = $(30 / 40) \times 100 = \mathbf{75.00\%}$
Future Classes Planned
  • Will attend = 8
  • Will miss = 2
  • New Total = $40 + 8 + 2 = \mathbf{50}$
  • New Attended = $30 + 8 = \mathbf{38}$
  • Predicted % = $(38 / 50) \times 100 = \mathbf{76.00\%}$
